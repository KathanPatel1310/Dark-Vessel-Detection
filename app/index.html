INTELLIGENCE OVERVIEW

What is Dark Vessel Detection?

Dark vessels — ships that disable or spoof their Automatic Identification System (AIS) transponders — represent one of the most significant threats to maritime security. Whether engaged in illegal fishing, sanctions evasion, or smuggling, these vessels deliberately conceal their movements from conventional tracking systems.

Our platform leverages Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) satellite imagery combined with cutting-edge deep learning to detect vessel signatures regardless of AIS status — piercing through weather, darkness, and deliberate deception.
